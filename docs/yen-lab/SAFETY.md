# Yên Lab · Short safety and claims copy

Issue: [#127](https://github.com/vanphamvn/yen-nghi-choi/issues/127). Editorial proposal checked 19/11/2026 by Codex research agent. Apply with the tool-specific [evidence matrix](EVIDENCE.md); this file does not approve implementation or clinical use.

## Shared copy

- Intro/detail: **“Một bài thực hành ngắn để bạn thử. Không phải công cụ chẩn đoán hay điều trị.”**
- Evidence detail: **“Nghiên cứu nói về kỹ thuật nền; bản thực hành này chưa được kiểm chứng hiệu quả riêng.”**
- Controls: **“Bắt đầu” · “Tạm dừng” · “Dừng lại” · “Bỏ qua” · “Thử lại”**. Never hide stop behind a modal or require completion.
- Completion: **“Mình dừng ở đây nhé.”** Optional feeling check must include “Không rõ” and “Bỏ qua”; never interpret it as a health score or evidence of efficacy.
- Creative label: **“Một trò nhỏ để thử.”** Do not attach an evidence badge merely because a general mindfulness link exists.

## Breathing Alpha

Before start: **“Thử thở nhẹ theo nhịp, trong mức dễ chịu.”**

Visible caution: **“Không cần hít sâu hay nín thở. Nếu chóng mặt, hụt hơi hoặc khó chịu, hãy dừng và thở tự nhiên.”**

Offer own pace and observing the circle without synchronizing breathing. No rapid breathing, retention, forced depth, success score, streak or reward for tolerating discomfort. Rhythm settings are product choices, not prescribed doses. Do not require closed eyes. Pause on hidden tab; do not race to catch up missed breaths on return.

## Muscle relaxation

**“Chỉ căng nhẹ nếu thấy dễ chịu. Bỏ qua chỗ đang đau hoặc chấn thương. Không nín thở; dừng và thả lỏng nếu khó chịu.”**

Do not publish a routine until its specific movements and shortened protocol receive the EVIDENCE.md G02 review. No strain, maximal force, pain threshold, neck loading or medical claim.

## Reflection, gratitude and sensory tasks

Gratitude: **“Một điều cũng được. Hôm nay không nghĩ ra điều gì cũng được.”** Do not require three entries, cheerful reframing or gratitude toward someone who caused harm.

Writing: ordinary, low-stakes prompts only; no compelled trauma narrative. Stop/reset/exit must erase private text in memory and UI; never log, persist, submit or put it in URL/history. Show a truthful short privacy explanation only after implementation is checked.

Grounding: eyes open, no obligatory smell/taste/touch, no collecting unknown objects. Audio begins off and requires explicit play. All sensory tasks must support skipping and reduced motion.

## Placement and publication gate

Use one short non-treatment sentence in the detail/intro, with method limitations behind a clear “Nguồn phương pháp” disclosure. Keep the breathing or muscle caution immediately beside its start controls; do not hide it in a footer. The visitor must still see one clear main action and an always-available stop/skip path. These are layout requirements for implementation review, not proof of the current mockup's rendered usability.

The research foundation may be complete while an individual routine remains deferred. G02's shortened muscle protocol and all deferred rows in EVIDENCE.md stay unpublished until their specific review is recorded. Re-review exact prompts, cadence, audience and any changed benefit wording before the corresponding first public release. Record source date, reviewer and PR/SHA, then perform the separate functional/privacy/browser gates.

## Editorial review checks

Reject “đã được khoa học chứng minh” applied to a Yên game, guaranteed calm, treatment promises, diagnosis, brain-reset language, magical frequencies and equivalence between simulated and physical nature. Keep cautions next to the relevant start/interaction; put research limitations in a concise details disclosure. Do not turn every page into a medical warning screen.

Review the actual final Vietnamese text and interactive states, not this document alone. Record `reviewerType=agent`, reviewer identity, date and actual tested SHA; do not imply clinician review, user signoff or production success.
